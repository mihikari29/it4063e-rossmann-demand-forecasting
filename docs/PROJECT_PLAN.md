# Remaining Project Deliverables

The [proposal](PROPOSAL.md) defines the eight course modules. [Progress and results](PROGRESS.md)
records completed work; [decisions](DECISIONS.md) fixes the accepted methods. This roadmap describes
remaining deliverables and dependencies without authorizing their execution.

“Required” identifies course delivery. A supporting safeguard remains mandatory for its affected
output. Optional work includes on-demand inference, additional models/features, longer horizons,
cross-model inventory simulation, SHAP, Evidently, Spark or Docker; none should block required
work without a demonstrated approved need.

## Dependency order

1. Freeze any accepted method changes before protected final release. Keep the selected LightGBM
   trial A/180-round recipe, feature contract, uncertainty tables and inventory semantics intact.
2. Establish independently authorized input chains for scenario analysis, STL and final evaluation.
   Shared identities never transfer permission between those contexts.
3. Complete the pending independent reviews and remaining synthetic milestones. Passing fixtures
   does not replace review, human visual acceptance, integration or explicit phase closure.
4. Measure and approve final-replay resource use, then separately authorize rehearsal, mechanical
   protected-source staging and each chronological analytical release.
5. Review analytical results and monitoring, then complete reporting and delivery acceptance.

Synthetic-demo review/release planning and report outlines can progress independently of real-source
operations. The API and Streamlit share a release bundle; Streamlit uses direct Python calls.
Render remains a hosting candidate, without a selected service or authorized purchase.

## Final evaluation boundary

Issue at July 3 for July 4–17 and at July 17 for July 18–31, persisting each full H14 block before
revealing any outcome in it. The second scheduled fresh fit may use only history already released
through July 17. Keep frozen June Fit B transport explicitly unvalidated. Preserve two independent
inventory episodes with standing targets; they do not demonstrate continuous rolling 28-day operation.

The selected monitoring method uses daily and trailing 7/14-calendar-day descriptive summaries by
origin/horizon, with deterministic integrity/data-quality alerts. The broader warning and human
review policy in the [proposal](PROPOSAL.md#242-proposed-operational-alerts-and-human-retraining-review) is unvalidated
and unapproved. It cannot adapt the frozen replay or introduce new final-test thresholds.

## Completed foundation

Phases 0–12 cover repository/data foundations, preparation/EDA, fixed features, the three-model
ladder, development selection, uncertainty, synthetic scenarios, inventory simulation and local
services/API/dashboard. Their reusable methods remain in the [decisions](DECISIONS.md),
[field contracts](DATA_DICTIONARY.md) and retained [method specifications](../plans/completed/).
M1–M3 and M4-A synthetic fixture slices are integrated; scoped M3/M4-A technical closeouts are
complete. Overall M4 is incomplete: M4-B/C remain unimplemented and unauthorized; M5–M8 remain
outstanding. Phase 13 remains PLANNED / NOT APPROVED. Phase 14 delivery is incomplete.

Follow the [workflow](WORKFLOW.md) for proportionate checks and review. Keep active plans until
required review, integration and explicit closure; the roadmap grants no source, execution,
hosting or publication authority.
