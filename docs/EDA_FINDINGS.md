# Phase 2 Data Preparation and EDA Findings

## Scope and reproducibility

Findings below were produced from the verified Rossmann snapshot dated 2026-10-05. The source
contains historical train data and a separate future-covariate test period. The reusable commands
are `python scripts/prepare_data.py` followed by `python scripts/run_eda.py`; the two numbered
notebooks call those same functions. Environment, source/output checksums, schemas, and package
versions are in ignored `data/interim/preparation_manifest.json` and `reports/eda/eda_summary.json`.
The full affected-store lists, row-level anomaly contexts, summary tables, and figure files remain
local under `reports/eda/` and are regenerated by the commands.

`Sales` is monetary turnover at Store × Date level. The train source was not changed. EDA is
descriptive: comparisons are not causal claims or forecast evaluations, and the project is not
SKU- or physical-unit-level.

### Historical source exposure and later modeling

Phase 1 source validation and this Phase 2 EDA inspected all labeled historical dates through
2015-07-31, including the period later protected for final forecasting evaluation. Full-source
percentiles, volume tiers, representative-store selection, and figures below are preserved
descriptive evidence, not proof that final-period labels were never inspected. No forecasting
holdout score was produced by those analyses.

Since the locked Phase 3 feature contract, modeling audits and selection use development data only,
with the [final-evaluation firewall](FEATURE_CONTRACT.md#final-holdout-firewall). Recompute model
volume cohorts, representative choices used in selection, preprocessing references, and monitoring
references from the relevant origin training/development period. Do not reuse the full-period EDA
tiers or Sales summaries to define a model-selection population or calibrate later policies.

## Preparation and source coverage

- Prepared historical train contains all 1,017,209 observed source rows (1,115 stores; 2013-01-01
  through 2015-07-31), including 172,817 closed rows. Prepared test contains 41,088 separate
  covariate rows (856 stores; 2015-08-01 through 2015-09-17) and has no `Sales` or `Customers`.
  Both tables retain unique Store × Date keys and pass many-to-one store metadata joins without
  row multiplication.
- The 180-store shared gap is exactly 2014-07-01 through 2014-12-31 (184 days; 33,120 absent
  store-days). All 180 stores have observations on 2014-06-30 and 2015-01-01, with dataset-wide
  observed boundaries of 2013-01-01 and 2015-07-31. The other 935 stores have rows throughout the
  interval. The affected cohort includes 84 StoreType-a, 81 StoreType-d, 14 StoreType-c, and one
  StoreType-b store; 156 participate in Promo2. Full IDs and store-level boundaries are in the
  ignored coverage diagnostics. One additional store, 988, is missing only the first dataset date
  (2013-01-01), a boundary omission rather than an internal gap.
- The canonical historical table remains sparse. The diagnostics record missing intervals without
  adding calendar rows or treating missing Sales/Open as zero. EDA daily aggregates retain actual
  daily source coverage so the six-month dip is visible rather than bridged.
- The 259 metadata stores unused by test are an expected Kaggle cohort difference. All 856 test
  stores join exactly once; no metadata store is dropped from the source.

## Missingness and anomaly dispositions

- Store metadata `Promo2SinceWeek`, `Promo2SinceYear`, and `PromoInterval` each have 544 nulls,
  exactly the 544 `Promo2 = 0` stores. All 571 participating stores have these details. Preserve
  these structural non-participation nulls.
- `CompetitionOpenSinceMonth` and `CompetitionOpenSinceYear` are jointly null for 354 stores; no
  partial pairs occur. The source does not establish the reason, so both remain missing and the
  cause remains unexplained. `CompetitionDistance` is missing for three stores; preserve those
  isolated nulls. No metadata imputation or model-specific missing indicator was introduced.
- The 54 `Open = 1, Sales = 0` observations span 41 stores; 52 also have zero Customers and two
  have positive Customers. All have source `StateHoliday = 0`; 12 coincide with `SchoolHoliday = 1`.
  The local row report includes current and previous/next observed `Open`, Sales, Customers, Promo,
  and holiday values, plus whether each neighbor is the consecutive calendar day. The records are
  retained; the available evidence does not confirm a correction or justify labeling them invalid.
- The maximum Sales observation remains $41,551 (Store 909, 2015-06-22; Promo=0, no coded state or
  school holiday, 1,721 Customers). It is retained as a review candidate, not classified as an
  error. The local context report also lists the lowest positive open-day observations and their
  neighboring source records.

### Missing future `Open` for Store 622

The canonical prepared test table preserves all 11 source nulls. Store 622 was historically open
for 94.1%–100% of known observations on weekdays and Saturdays and closed on all 134 known Sunday
observations. Each missing test date is a weekday or Saturday. For every missing row, earlier
Store 622 history has at least 43 exact matches on `DayOfWeek`, `Promo`, `StateHoliday`, and
`SchoolHoliday`; each set is unanimous `Open = 1` (43–134 matching rows, 100% observed open).

The separate `test_open_resolution.parquet` therefore records `Open_resolved = 1` for these 11
rows under the deterministic rule “at least 30 earlier exact-context rows, unanimous Open,” with
`resolution_uncertain = true` and match counts/rates. This is a historical-context candidate, not
observed future status or guaranteed ground truth. The source `Open` column remains null, and the
candidate rule uses no future Sales or Customers. Phase 3 subsequently resolved the downstream
policy in accepted ADR-012 and the [Feature Contract](FEATURE_CONTRACT.md): these candidates remain
audit-only, never establish evaluation eligibility, and never force an operational zero. Missing
source Open remains unknown rather than a known closure.

## Descriptive Sales patterns

- Across all 1,017,209 historical rows, median Sales is 5,744 and the 99th percentile is 17,160.
  On the primary descriptive subset `Open = 1` (844,392 rows), median Sales is 6,369 and the 90th
  percentile is 10,771. There are 172,871 zero-Sales rows in total: all 172,817 closed rows plus
  the 54 open/zero-Sales observations.
- Source weekday code medians on open rows range from 5,425 (code 6; 144,058 rows) to 8,224.7
  (code 7; 3,593 rows across only 33 stores). The sparse code-7 denominator makes unqualified
  weekday comparisons misleading; source codes are left uninterpreted in the report.
- Open-day raw medians are 5,459 for `Promo = 0` and 7,649 for `Promo = 1`. Averaging each store's
  open-day Sales separately by promotion status and then across stores gives mean store means of
  about 5,907 and 8,205, respectively; all 1,115 stores have observations in both groups. These
  are observed associations, not promotion effects.
- Open-day row medians differ across StoreType codes: a=6,285 (602 stores), b=9,130 (17), c=6,407
  (148), d=6,395 (348). By Assortment code: a=6,082 (593 stores), b=8,081 (9), c=6,675 (513).
  Small strata, especially StoreType-b/Assortment-b, warrant caution. Store-level summaries and
  counts are also provided to avoid treating every store-day as an independent store.
- Store-level mean open-day Sales were ranked deterministically into approximate low/middle/high
  thirds. The resulting groups contain 371/372/372 stores, with median store means of about
  4,886 / 6,589 / 8,742. Median observed open-day coverage is 779 days per store, reflecting the
  common gap and store-specific closures.
- Median store-level mean Sales is about 6,936 for Promo2 nonparticipants (544 stores) and 6,267
  for participants (571). Competition-distance summaries and plots are descriptive only; the
  source unit is not asserted, three distances are missing, and no causal effect is inferred.
- State-holiday categories are sparse among open rows: the `b` category has only six observations
  across one store. Holiday summaries show counts with medians and do not treat sparse categories
  as stable comparisons. Monthly/year summaries and daily sales/coverage figures are available in
  local CSV/JSON outputs.

## Deterministic representative stores and serial diagnostics

Stores were selected from per-store mean Sales over observed open days by nearest distance to the
10th, 50th, and 90th percentiles, then by a deterministic greedy cover of StoreType, Assortment,
and Promo2 categories (ties by Store ID). One shared-gap example and the lowest-Store-ID
open/zero-Sales anomaly example were added, then IDs were deduplicated. The resulting set is:

| Store | Selection role |
|---:|---|
| 22 | nearest p10 open-day store mean |
| 670 | nearest p50 open-day store mean |
| 368 | nearest p90 open-day store mean |
| 371, 542, 676, 1044 | greedy metadata-category coverage |
| 13 | shared date-gap diagnostic |
| 25 | open/zero-Sales diagnostic |

ACF diagnostics use each representative's longest uninterrupted observed daily Sales segment only;
no values are filled to span a gap. The selected segment lengths range from 546 to 942 days. Lag-7
autocorrelation varies across examples (about 0.27–0.75), consistent with meaningful but
heterogeneous weekly serial structure. No decomposition or forecasting model was fitted.

Fourteen reproducible figures and compact tables are generated under `reports/eda/`. Detailed
per-store IDs, missingness overlaps, exact anomaly contexts, ACF values, and group denominators
remain in the ignored machine-readable report. Future work must preserve sparse chronological
coverage, exclude future Customers, keep primary modeling/evaluation restricted to actual
`Open = 1`, and keep the chronological final period excluded from model/feature selection and
policy calibration under the disclosed firewall.
